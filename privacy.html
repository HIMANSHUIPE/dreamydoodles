Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

This policy covers KidsPoems Uploader ("the app"), a private tool operated by DreamyDoodles that uploads videos to the operator's own YouTube channel through the YouTube API Services.

Who uses the app

Only the channel owner signs in. The app is not available to the public, has no user accounts, and does not collect information from viewers, children or any other person.

What data the app accesses

  • With the owner's permission (Google OAuth), the app uses the scopes youtube.upload (to upload videos) and youtube.readonly (to confirm which channel it is uploading to and read back the status of uploaded videos).
  • It sends the owner's own video files and their titles, descriptions and settings to YouTube.

How data is stored and shared

  • The sign-in token is stored only on the owner's own computer. It is not sent to DreamyDoodles servers (there are none) or to any third party.
  • No data is sold, shared, or used for advertising. No analytics or tracking are used.
  • Data is only sent to Google/YouTube, to perform the uploads the owner requests.

Google and YouTube

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using it, you are also subject to the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at Google security settings → third-party access. Deleting the token file on the owner's computer also removes the app's stored access.

Children

The videos uploaded are made for children and are marked "made for kids" on YouTube. The app itself does not collect any personal information from children or anyone else.
